Help

How to add and change things on each page. The ? button on a page opens its part.

Pages

Today

What needs you today, what is on, and the money ahead, for the whole family or one person.

Add something

  • Got a paper? Press Add or scan and drop the bill, statement, receipt or photo. It is read, filed and its due date lands here.
  • Rather type it? Press Chat and write it the way you would say it, for example "Sam's dentist is Oct 21 at 3pm". You see what will be saved and press Yes, save it.
  • Birthdays, classes, IDs and other dates have their own forms in Records (links below).

Done, paid, snooze

  • Press Done or Paid on a row under Needs you. A paid bill shows Undo for a moment.
  • To snooze or hide something, open To do and use the ⋯ menu on its row.

Change or correct

  • Open the row in To do (⋯ > Open record) or find it in Records; the forms under it change it.

Tips

  • Tap a name above the card to see only that person's things.
  • On a phone, tap or swipe the card for On today and Money.

Family

One card per person (and one for the house and cars) with their next 30 days.

Add a person or a pet

  • Go to Records > People > People and use Add a person or pet: name, role, birthday.

Change or correct

  • The pencil on a card opens that person's record: name, birthday, school, IDs. Fill in Change it and press Save changes.
  • A new name is used everywhere from then on; papers already filed keep the old spelling.

See one person

  • Tap a card to list everything for that person. From there, open Today or To do just for them.

Records > People

Calendar

Every due date, appointment, class, holiday and birthday from your records, by week, month or year.

Add something

  • The calendar shows your records, so you add things where they belong:
  • a weekly class or sport: Records > People > Classes and activities, Add a weekly class or activity;
  • a holiday or a day off school: Records > People > Holidays and dates;
  • a doctor, school or pet date: Records > People > Life items, Add an item;
  • a bill: drop it in the Inbox, or Finance > Bills. Or just tell Chat.

Change or correct

  • Change the record it comes from (Records, or Finance for bills). The calendar updates by itself.

Moving around

  • Week, Month and Year switch the view; the arrows and Today move through time.
  • In the year view, tap a coloured day to open its week.

Tips

  • The card on top counts what is on, who it is for and what not to miss.
  • Your Google calendar, if connected, is shown below as a read-only copy.

Inbox

The way in for every paper: drop files here, watch them being read, then answer what needs a person.

Add files

  • Drag files anywhere onto this page, paste a picture, or press Choose files or take a photo.
  • Each file shows a bar while it is sent. Scans from the phone app arrive here too.

Watch them being read

  • The panel under the drop box shows every file and its step: in line, checking, reading, sorting, filed.
  • With an AI helper set up, reading starts on its own. Otherwise press File them now: common bank statements, CSV exports and phone bills are read without AI. Anything it cannot read waits under Files to look at.

Work through what is left

  • Open items are grouped: Files to look at, Money, Renewals and documents, Family and household, Calendar. Tap a group name at the top, or press Work through these, to see only that group.
  • Each card asks one thing (a new subscription, a price rise, a missing birthday ...). Pick an answer and press Save, or snooze it for later. After filing, Open record shows what was saved.

Tips

  • An exact copy of a paper already filed is set aside, never filed twice.

This month

Where the money went this month (or any month), plus bills, subscriptions and upkeep coming up.

Fill it

  • Spending comes from your bank and card statements: drop them in the Inbox (Add or scan).
  • Budget lines: Finance > Budget (Set or remove a budget line). Subscriptions: Finance > Subscriptions (Add a subscription).

Read the card

  • Tap or swipe through Overview, Cash flow, Where it went, Six months and Highlights.
  • Pick another month with the month list or the arrows.
  • Below the card, open a category to see every charge, biggest first or by date.

Change or correct

  • Charges come straight from your statements and are not edited here. Bills and subscriptions are changed in Finance.

Wallet

Your cards in one place: IDs, cars and plates, insurance, memberships and home facts.

Add

  • IDs and insurance come from filed papers and Records; cars from Records > Vehicles.
  • Open Add a card for a membership, store, library, travel or health card, or home facts (Wi-Fi name, trash day, filter size), then Save card.
  • On a car, Plate and colour keeps the whole plate, its state, colour and body type; then Save.
  • Add a card from a photo: take or pick a photo of the card. When photo reading is on (Settings > Connections > AI helper) the AI fills in the card (only the last four of a number); check every field, then Save card, or Discard the photo. The photo is filed with the card and shown on its back.

Change

  • Under a card you added: Change to correct it, or tick the box and Remove.
  • IDs, cars and policies change in Records: tap the card, then Open in Records.

Find

  • Tap a card to see where its number is printed on the real one, and your own scan of it when one is filed. The chips at the top show one group; a card running out within 60 days says how many days are left.
  • Type in the search box (plate, wifi, library, a name) and Find: every word must be on the card.
  • Parking shows the plate as big as the screen allows for a meter or toll booth, with Copy the plate; the screen stays on. Installed as an app, press and hold its icon for the Plate shortcut.
  • A card you added with an expiry date is reminded 30 days before in To do and the weekly digest (Settings > Household profile > How early to remind changes it).

Tips

  • These are reference copies, not official IDs. Whole numbers are kept only for the plate and for membership, store and library cards (for the barcode); everything else keeps the last 4.
  • Paired phones with the ID documents permission carry the wallet too.

Notes

Links, notes, places and deals you want to find again.

Add

  • Open Save a link, note, place or deal, type a title (and the link if there is one), pick who sees it, then Save.
  • A deal with an end date also shows up in To do before it ends.

Change

  • Pinned items sit on top as coloured cards with their buttons; on any other row tap ... to see them.
  • Pin keeps a note on top; Unpin lets it go back in line.
  • Tags changes its tags (Save tags); Archive puts it away.

Find

  • Type in Search, or tap a kind in the row under it (Places, Deals ...). More filters has the tag, who added it and Show archived and ended deals. A deal ending within two weeks shows how many days are left.

Tips

  • Links are never opened by the app; they open in a new tab only when you click them.

Chat

Ask about your records, or tell it something to save, in plain words.

Ask

  • "When does the car insurance renew?", "What did we spend on groceries last month?". Each answer says which records it used.

Add or change

  • Type it the way you would say it: "Sam's dentist is Oct 21 at 3pm". Nothing is saved until you press Yes, save it on the card that shows exactly what will change.

Tips

  • Typed requests need an AI helper (Settings > Connections). Clear this chat starts over.
  • Answers are records, not professional advice.

Work

Contracts you bill by the hour: hours per timesheet period, invoices, the on / off-site schedule and the work calendar.

Add

  • Add a contract at the top: client, rate, how often you invoice and the timesheet cycle.
  • Hours come from a timesheet you import, or are typed per day under Hours.

Change

  • Pick the contract's part in the row of chips: Billing, Hours, Schedule, Profile, Work calendar.
  • Billing: Record invoice for the month makes the month's invoice; mark it sent or paid below and Save.

Tips

  • Days on the schedule with no hours, and hours on a holiday, show in red above the weeks.
  • Off-site days feed the home-office figures in Finance; whether a deduction applies is for your tax preparer.

Settings

Your household, the agent, connections, phones and your data.

Find your way

  • Settings opens on a summary: how complete the profile is (Answer next asks the next question), one card per group with how it stands, and Needs a look for health warnings. Open a group, then switch groups in the row at the top; tap a card's heading to open it.

Household profile

  • How complete your profile is, Simple or Advanced view, names as printed on papers, traditions and holidays, optional features, tax papers and how early to remind.

Agent

  • The agent's name and tone, when the daily brief and weekly digest arrive, and what they watch; sign-in PINs (Save PIN); items hidden from To do (Restore).

Connections

  • An AI helper, Google Calendar and Gmail, and the phone bot. Keys can be typed only on this PC.
  • Pro: Bank sync (SimpleFIN) pulls balances and new transactions every morning. Paste the setup token on this PC, tick the accounts (Select all / Unselect all), save, then Pull now.

Phones

  • Pair a phone with the app's QR code (Show the pairing code); remove a lost phone.

Data

  • Back up, restore, export, updates, the health check, and Runs on its own (the daily brief, weekly digest and backup on a schedule).
