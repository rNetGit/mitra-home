Help

How to add and change things on each page. The ? button on a page opens its part.

Pages

Today

What needs you today, what is on, and the money ahead, for the whole family or one person.

Add something

  • Got a paper? Press Add or scan and drop the bill, statement, receipt or photo. It is read, filed and its due date lands here.
  • Rather type it? Press Chat and write it the way you would say it, for example "Sam's dentist is Oct 21 at 3pm". You see what will be saved and press Yes, save it.
  • Birthdays, classes, IDs and other dates have their own forms in Family (links below).

Done, paid, snooze

  • Press Done or Paid on a row under Late, Today or This week. A paid bill shows Undo for a moment.
  • The ⋯ menu on a row: snooze it 1, 7 or 30 days, or hide it. Hidden items come back from Settings > Agent > Hidden from Today (Restore).

Change or correct

  • ⋯ > Open record on a row, or find it in Family; the forms under it change it.

Documents

  • Under Documents: This week, Weekly digest, Family meeting and Emergency sheet make a page you can read here, download or print.
  • Under the emergency sheet: Where things are (the will, who can open the password manager, the safe, the backup drive ...; places and names only) is printed on the sheet (Save where things are). Download the sealed copy makes one file for someone you trust that opens in any browser with a passphrase you tell them separately.

Tips

  • Tap a name above the card to see only that person's things.
  • On a phone, tap or swipe the card for On today and Money.
  • With the ZIP code set and Look things up online on (Family > House), the line under the title shows today's weather.

Family

One card per person (and one for the house and cars) with their next 30 days, a search over every record and filed paper, and Needs a look (overdue items, IDs and car renewals running out, policies ending, upkeep due). The chips at the top open People, Home, Cars, Papers & IDs and the Wallet.

Add a person or a pet

  • Go to Family > People > People and use Add a person or pet: name, role, birthday.

Change or correct

  • The pencil on a card opens that person's record: name, birthday, school, IDs. Fill in Change it and press Save changes.
  • A new name is used everywhere from then on; papers already filed keep the old spelling.

See one person

  • Tap a card to list everything for that person. From there, open Today just for them.

The house

  • The pencil on the House card opens Family > House: city, state and ZIP code (no street address). The state picks the state holidays on the calendar.
  • Look things up online (off until you turn it on) lets the app list schools near home, check a college's name, find this year's school, college or work calendar and show the local weather. Only the ZIP code, the state, public names and the school year go out; never anyone's name.

School, college or work

  • On a person's record, under School, college or work calendar: Schools near home lists the schools around your ZIP code (Pick fills one in); Check name catches a typo in a college's name.
  • Find this year's calendar asks your AI to search the web for the official calendar; Import one it found, or use Import from link or Import file. The days off wait in the Inbox until you add them.

Children's jobs

  • Family > Routines: the children's morning, after-school and evening jobs with stars, their goals and the allowance. They show on the Mitra Wall.

Calendar

Every due date, appointment, class, holiday and birthday from your records, by week, month or year.

Add something

  • The calendar shows your records, so you add things where they belong:
  • a weekly class or sport: Family > People > Classes and activities, Add a weekly class or activity;
  • a holiday or a day off school: Family > People > Holidays and dates;
  • a family event at a time (dinner 8 to 8:30 PM, a party) or a yearly date: the same page, Add a family event or a yearly date, or tell Chat "family dinner today 8 to 8:30 pm";
  • a doctor, school or pet date: Family > People > Life items, Add an item;
  • a bill: drop it in the Inbox, or Money > Pay > Bills. Or just tell Chat.

Change or correct

  • Change the record it comes from (Family, or Money for bills). The calendar updates by itself.

Moving around

  • Week, Month and Year switch the view; the arrows and Today move through time.
  • In the year view, tap a coloured day to open its week.

Tips

  • The card on top counts what is on, who it is for and what not to miss.
  • Your Google calendar, if connected, is shown below as a read-only copy; so are other calendars (iCloud, Outlook, school, work) added in Settings > Connections > Other calendars.
  • If the app puts its dates on a Google calendar of its own and someone moves or deletes one there, the Inbox asks you: use the new date, keep ours, or take it off. Nothing changes until you answer.

Add

The way in for everything: drop or scan papers, type something in, and the Inbox of what needs a person.

Type it in

  • The tiles under Or type it in open the form where that thing lives: a person or pet, a class, a date or reminder, a family event, a list item, a note, a subscription, a card for the wallet, or Chat.
  • Dates from a photo: take a photo of a flyer, invitation or school letter. With photo reading on, the AI lists its dates; check each line (day, name, time, who, kind), untick what you do not want, then Add the ticked dates. Without the AI, type them next to the photo.
  • Recipe from a photo: a recipe card or cookbook page fills in the recipe; check it, then Save the recipe.

Add files

  • Drag files anywhere onto this page, paste a picture, or press Choose files or take a photo.
  • Each file shows a bar while it is sent. Scans from the phone app arrive here too.

Watch them being read

  • The panel under the drop box shows every file and its step: in line, checking, reading, sorting, filed.
  • With an AI helper set up, reading starts on its own. Otherwise press File them now: common bank statements, CSV exports and phone bills are read without AI. Anything it cannot read waits under Files to look at.
  • Each waiting file says why, and Why? shows every step that was tried. Add the account ending ... adds the account a statement names and reads it again; Try again reads a file again after you changed something (photo reading on, AI account topped up); Try all again does it for every file.

Work through what is left

  • Open items are grouped: Files to look at, Money, Renewals and documents, Family and household, Calendar. Tap a group name at the top, or press Work through these, to see only that group.
  • Each card asks one thing (a new subscription, a price rise, a missing birthday ...). Pick an answer and press Save, or snooze it for later. After filing, Open record shows what was saved.

Tips

  • An exact copy of a paper already filed is set aside, never filed twice.

Routines

The children's jobs on the Mitra Wall: morning, after school and evening, with stars.

Add

  • Under Add a job pick the child, when, the job, a picture and its stars (1 to 5), tick the days it is on (none = every day), then Add the job.
  • On the wall a child taps the job when it is done; tapping again takes it back. Each job counts once a day.
  • A child who signs in with their own PIN also sees My jobs today on their page and ticks them there (only their own jobs, with their stars but never an amount).

Rewards

  • Under Rewards: what a child can spend stars on, for one child or any child, then Add the reward.
  • A child asks on the wall's Chores screen or their own page; Today tells you, and you answer under Asked for: Give it spends the stars, Not now gives them back.
  • Stars to spend = every star earned, minus rewards asked for or given. The allowance does not use them up.

Change

  • Remove takes a job off the wall; the stars it already earned stay in the week.
  • What a star is worth: cents per star, then Save the rate. 0 = stars only.

Tips

  • Stars count Monday to Sunday. The week's allowance shows on the wall's Money screen, which opens only with an adult's PIN; nothing is paid automatically.
  • Pair the wall itself in Settings > Phones > Mitra Wall.

Mitra Wall

An old tablet or iPad on the wall that shows the family's week, today's plan, the children's jobs, the lists and the meals.

Set it up

  • On this PC: Settings > Phones > Mitra Wall, name it, Show the wall code, then scan the code with the tablet's camera. docs/MITRA_WALL.md has the steps to keep it on the wall (Guided Access, screen pinning).

What it shows

  • Today: what is on, what needs doing (the same to-dos as Today on the PC, late ones marked), the children's jobs, dinner and Coming up (the next three days).
  • Week and Month at the top switch the calendar; tap a person to see only their day. Tap anything to see it in full: the whole title, the day and time and who it is for.
  • Chores, Lists and Meals: children tick their jobs, anyone ticks or adds to the family lists.
  • After two minutes untouched it rests on a clock and the family's photos (Settings > Phones > Mitra Wall > Wall screen).

Money stays off it

  • Bills show as due or late, never with an amount. Money opens only with an adult's own PIN and locks itself after a minute.

Tips

  • With the ZIP code and Look things up online on (Family > House) it shows the local weather; the PC fetches it, the tablet never goes online.
  • Adults-only lists, critical info and papers never show on the wall.

Wallet

Your cards in one place: IDs, cars and plates, insurance, memberships and home facts.

Add

  • IDs and insurance come from filed papers and Family; cars from Family > Cars.
  • Open Add a card for a membership, store, library, travel or health card, or home facts (Wi-Fi name, trash day, filter size), then Save card.
  • On a car, Plate and colour keeps the whole plate, its state, colour and body type; then Save.
  • Add a card from a photo: take or pick a photo of the card. When photo reading is on (Settings > Connections > AI helper) the AI fills in the card (only the last four of a number); check every field, then Save card, or Discard the photo. The photo is filed with the card and shown on its back.

Change

  • Under a card you added: Change to correct it, or tick the box and Remove.
  • IDs, cars and policies change in Family: tap the card, then Open record.

Find

  • Tap a card to see where its number is printed on the real one, and your own scan of it when one is filed. The chips at the top show one group; a card running out within 60 days says how many days are left.
  • Type in the search box (plate, wifi, library, a name) and Find: every word must be on the card.
  • Parking shows the plate as big as the screen allows for a meter or toll booth, with Copy the plate; the screen stays on. Installed as an app, press and hold its icon for the Plate shortcut.
  • A card you added with an expiry date is reminded 30 days before on Today and in the weekly digest (Settings > Household profile > How early to remind changes it).

Tips

  • These are reference copies, not official IDs. Whole numbers are kept only for the plate and for membership, store and library cards (for the barcode); everything else keeps the last 4.
  • Paired phones with the ID documents permission carry the wallet too.

Notes

Add

Change

Chat

Ask about your records, or tell it something to save, in plain words.

Ask

  • "When does the car insurance renew?", "What did we spend on groceries last month?". Each answer says which records it used.

Add or change

  • Type it the way you would say it: "Sam's dentist is Oct 21 at 3pm". Nothing is saved until you press Yes, save it on the card that shows exactly what will change.

Tips

  • Typed requests need an AI helper (Settings > Connections). Clear this chat starts over.
  • Answers are records, not professional advice.

Settings

Your household, the agent, connections, phones and your data.

Find your way

  • Settings opens on a summary: how complete the profile is (Ask me the next question puts it in the Inbox), one card per group with how it stands, and Needs a look for health warnings. Open a group, then switch groups in the row at the top; tap a card's heading to open it.

Household profile

  • How complete your profile is, Simple or Advanced view, names as printed on papers, traditions and holidays, optional features, tax papers and how early to remind.

Agent

  • The agent's name and tone, when the daily brief and weekly digest arrive, and what they watch; sign-in PINs (Save PIN); items hidden from Today (Restore).

Connections

  • An AI helper, Google Calendar and Gmail, and the phone bot. Keys can be typed only on this PC.
  • Mail folder: a mailbox made only for this app (any provider) that you forward papers to; type its address and an app password on this PC, then Save. It is read with every inbox check, read-only (docs/MAIL_FOLDER.md).
  • Other calendars: paste an iCloud, Outlook or other calendar's subscribe address (.ics or webcal://) and Add the calendar; it shows on the Calendar page, read-only, read about once an hour.
  • Pro: Bank sync (SimpleFIN) pulls balances and new transactions every morning. Paste the setup token on this PC, tick the accounts (Select all / Unselect all), save, then Pull now.

Phones

  • Pair a phone with the app's QR code (Show the pairing code); remove a lost phone.
  • Mitra Wall: an old tablet or iPad on the wall. Name it, Show the wall code, then scan the code with the tablet's camera (or open the address on it and type the code within 15 minutes). Remove a wall to sign it out.
  • Wall screen: after two minutes untouched the wall shows a big clock (digital, traditional, modular = the time with the weather, what is next, the children's jobs and dinner, or Roman = hands with serif numerals) and in the day the family's photos in turn; at night a dark clock, or with Night clock red or amber a bedroom clock (black, only the time and the date). Pick the clock, the night clock, the photo and night hours and how long each shows, then Save the wall screen.
  • Add photos (several at once, iPhone photos too): each is made again on this PC, upright and without its location or camera details. They stay on this PC; only a paired wall shows them.
  • Away from home: with Tailscale on the PC and the phone, Settings > Phones shows an Away from home address to open in the phone's browser (docs/REMOTE_ACCESS.md). The PC is never open to the internet.
  • A phone that stops connecting after the PC renews its own certificate (about once a year): pair it again here. Phone app 0.14 and later stay paired.

Data

  • Back up, restore, export, updates, the health check, and Runs on its own (the daily brief, weekly digest and backup on a schedule).
  • Data check reads the records every week for charges entered twice, bills still open long after their due date, phone bills that do not add up and papers missing on disk; Check now runs it at once. The safe fix (a missing category one of your rules knows) happens by itself and can be undone there; charges or bills entered twice and bills still open wait in the Inbox to accept or leave. With an AI helper set up, Explain with AI puts the findings in plain words.

Ledger

Every transaction from every account in one list, with the money flow worked out above it.

Add

  • Drop the statements in the Inbox. For a checking account, drop the latest statement or activity download; the page tells you which accounts are behind.

Change or correct

  • Pick a period (the dates fill in) or type your own dates, narrow by account, category, type, amount or a word, then press Show.
  • One card: pick it under Account; By card member splits its spending per person. Category Uncategorized lists the card shops with no category: name one and press Remember, and every charge from it is sorted from then on.
  • A line in the wrong category: open it, pick the category (and the type), then Save. Tick Do the same for every line like this one and the same shop is sorted that way from then on.
  • The lines themselves come from your statements and are not edited here.

The tiles

  • Income: pay and interest. Spending: everything bought, billed or charged, with refunds taken off. Left over: income less spending (and tax).
  • Bills paid is the part of spending that is utilities, insurance, loans, phone and internet. Invested is money set aside, such as a 529 or a brokerage deposit.
  • Between your accounts: money you moved from one of your accounts to another. Card payments: money paid onto your cards. Neither is income or spending, so they never inflate those tiles, except in a month with no card statement: then a card payment stands for that card's spending, and a note says so.
  • A transfer or card payment is matched when the same amount goes out of one account and into another within three days. Each payment is counted once.

The yellow notes

  • Not up to date names the accounts whose newest line is older than the dates you picked. Their spending, pay and transfers are missing until you add the latest statement. A salary account that is behind is why Income can show nothing.
  • Other side not loaded: a transfer or card payment whose other end is not in the list yet, or is an account you do not track. It matches by itself once that statement is added.

Check the totals

  • Open How the tiles add up to see every line grouped, with the totals in and out. Pick a kind of line under it (Spending, Transfers, Card payments, Investing...) to list just those.
