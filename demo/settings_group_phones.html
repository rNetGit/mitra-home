Mitra Wall

An old tablet or iPad on the wall that shows the week, today's plan, the children's jobs, the lists and the meals. It opens Money only when an adult types their own PIN, and closes it again after a minute.

Kitchen wall · paired Wed, Oct 21 · last seen Wed, Oct 21 13:02

The code works once, for 15 minutes. The wall can tick family lists and the children's jobs (set them in Family › Routines); it never sees adults-only lists, critical info or papers.

Wall screen

After two minutes untouched the wall shows a full-size clock, and in the day the family's photos in turn. At night it stays on a dark clock.
