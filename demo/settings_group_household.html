Names on your papers

Full names hidden before anything goes to the AI

Bills, policies and statements often print full legal names, not the short names you use here. Write them one per line, so they are hidden before anything is sent to the AI. They stay on this PC.

Optional features

Packs you can switch on

Switch on only what you will use. Turning one on never removes anything.

Traditions and holidays

Festivals and holidays on the calendar

Public holidays: US federal holidays + Illinois (switch them off under Optional features). Optional: the traditions your family keeps (religious, cultural or regional), one per line, so their festivals and holidays reach the calendar. They stay on this PC: never on phones, Drive or a chat bot.

The look-up needs an AI: no AI provider is set up (Settings > Connections > AI).

Tax papers

Payers, state tax, tuition

Names only, separated by commas. No account numbers. All three are optional and stay on this PC.
