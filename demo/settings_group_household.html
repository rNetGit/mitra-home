Names on your papers

Full names hidden before anything goes to the AI

Bills, policies and statements often print full legal names, not the short names you use here. Write them one per line, so they are hidden before anything is sent to the AI. They stay on this PC.

Public holidays: US federal holidays + Illinois (switch them off under the Advanced view's Optional features). Optional: the traditions your family keeps (religious, cultural or regional), one per line, so their festivals and holidays reach the calendar. They stay on this PC: never on phones, Drive or a chat bot.
