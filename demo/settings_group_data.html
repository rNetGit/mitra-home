Data check

Records that do not add up, entered twice or missing

Every week, with the weekly digest, Mitra Home reads through the records for charges entered twice, bills still open long after their due date, phone bills that do not add up and filed papers missing on disk. It fixes only what cannot be wrong, with Undo; what needs you waits in the Inbox. No AI is used.

Open the data check

Calendar file

Every due date for a phone's calendar

Download the calendar file (next 365 days)

Open it on your phone to add every due date to its calendar. It holds names, amounts and last-4 digits only; importing it again updates the events instead of doubling them.

Back up and export

Back up now or export every table
Working... this can take a minute.

A backup also runs every week by itself once it runs on its own (below). An export is every table as CSV files in one zip, for you to keep or open in a spreadsheet.

Restore from backup

Put the records back as they were

Puts the household's records back as they were at the time of a backup. Everything entered since then is replaced; a copy of today's data is kept first. Filed papers and settings are not overwritten, only ones that went missing come back. To undo a restore, pick the copy from just before it.

The phone bot pauses during the restore and starts again afterwards. This page closes for about a minute.

Updates

Check for and install new versions

This is Mitra Home 0.116.0.

Check for new versions every day

Once a day the morning task asks api.github.com for the latest release number and its notes. Only that address is contacted and nothing about your family is sent. A new version shows up in the Inbox; it is never installed without you. Off: no network call at all.

Full check

Every task on this PC and the AI

Also checks every scheduled task on this PC (not only this household's), and asks the AI a short question, so it uses a little of your AI plan (about a minute).
