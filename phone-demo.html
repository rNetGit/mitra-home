Phone app

Try the phone app without a PC

The Mitra Home phone app normally shows your family's papers from your own PC. To look around first, open it on a made-up household, the Riveras. No PC, no account, nothing leaves your phone.

  1. Open the Mitra Home phone app (Android or iPhone).
  2. On the first screen tap Scan the pairing code and point the camera at this code.
  3. Or skip the code: tap No PC? Try the demo.
  4. Leave it any time in Settings › Leave the demo.

In the demo you can look at Today, Calendar, Money, Family, lists and notes, tick things off and mark a bill paid. Changes stay on the phone for the length of the demo and are then discarded. Scanning papers, sending them, opening the PC page, the phone's own AI and the app lock are switched off.

Want to see the PC app too? Open the PC demo. Questions: cloudwareedge@gmail.com.
