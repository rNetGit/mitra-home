s1	Meet Mitra Home. Your family's bills, papers, money and calendar, kept on your own computer.
s2	Open it on your PC, and Today shows what needs you: what's late, what's due, and what's coming this week.
s3	Drop in a bill, a statement or a school letter. Mitra files it, and pulls out the dates and amounts, with the AI you choose.
s4	Everyone's appointments, classes, school days off and holidays, in one family calendar.
s5	Money works like your bank: every account, card, investment and loan, with what you have, what you owe, and your net worth.
s6	See where the money went each month, against last month and last year.
s7	Family keeps each person's records, the cars, IDs and insurance, and a wallet for the numbers you always look for.
s8	The free phone app, for Android and iPhone, pairs with your PC on home Wi-Fi. Check today, mark a bill paid, or add an event on the go.
s9	Turn an old tablet into the Mitra Wall: the family's week at a glance, the kids' jobs and stars, and what's for dinner.
s10	When no one is using it, it becomes Mitra Clock. Pick a face, add your photos, and at night it dims to a soft bedside clock.
s11	No subscription, and your records never leave home. Mitra Home is free and open source. Try the demo on our website.
